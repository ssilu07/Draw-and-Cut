Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Draw & Cut ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Draw & Cut does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App runs entirely offline on your device. We do not collect:

  • Personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Photos, media, or files

2. Internet Access

The App requires internet access to display advertisements via Google AdMob. It may also load external fonts (Google Fonts) for visual styling.

3. Data Storage

All game data (drawings, scores, progress) is stored locally on your device only. No data is uploaded to any server by the App itself.

4. Third-Party Services

Draw & Cut uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

The App does not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase (other than AdMob)
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting tools

5. Children's Privacy

Draw & Cut is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

6. Permissions

The App does not request any special device permissions (such as camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
